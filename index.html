The Voice of a People Demanding Freedom

In recent years, Iranians have taken to the streets to demand their most basic human rights — the right to live, to choose, and to build a better future. The response has been live ammunition, mass arrests, and severe sentences. This website exists to document the truth, honor the victims, and amplify the voices of families still searching for their loved ones.

The world must not look away.

Read Their Stories

In Memory of the Victims

Thousands of civilians — children, teenagers, women, and men — have lost their lives during the protests. Many were killed by live fire, and in some cases families faced pressure or financial demands to reclaim the bodies of their loved ones. Behind every name is a life, a dream, and a grieving family.

We are committed to ensuring they are never forgotten.

Detainees and Those at Risk of Execution

Thousands have been detained. Many families have had no news of their loved ones for weeks or months. Numerous reports point to torture, forced confessions, and the risk of execution. These individuals are imprisoned for demanding freedom and human dignity.

36,500+ Killed
300,000+ Injured

Many of the injured were shot in the face with shotgun pellets, leaving them permanently blinded. Families report that wounded protesters were arrested directly from hospitals.

Sources: Amnesty International, Human Rights Watch, HRANA, Iran Human Rights, family testimonies, verified video evidence

Future and Leadership

Many Iranians believe that moving beyond the Islamic Republic requires figures and movements capable of fostering national unity. Among them, Reza Pahlavi is recognized as a well-known advocate of democracy, secular governance, and a referendum by the people.

Ultimately, the final decision about Iran's future system of government must be made through the free will of its citizens.

Secular Democracy

Complete separation of religion and state, with elected representatives accountable to the people.

Human Rights

Full adherence to the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and international conventions.

Free Elections

A transitional government leading to free, fair, and internationally monitored elections.

Territorial Integrity

A unified Iran that respects and celebrates its ethnic and cultural diversity.
